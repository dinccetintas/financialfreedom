<objective>
Look at the supply chain before running the first `npm install` in the repository.

**User story (MVP framing, sourced from the ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal):** **As a** member of the family allowlist, **I want to** sign in, create a portfolio and record my first US stock purchase, **so that** what I own is recorded once, correctly, on a schema that will not have to be rebuilt under live data.

This plan is the first gate in front of that story. `01-RESEARCH.md` §Package Legitimacy Audit flags four packages `[SUS]` on a `too-new` publish date. The audit assesses the real risk as effectively nil — all four have multi-million weekly downloads and matching official GitHub repos — but the protocol is that a `[SUS]` package is confirmed by a human before it is first installed, and that confirmation is never auto-approvable regardless of `workflow.auto_advance`. A fifth package, `@react-email/components`, is `[SUS] deprecated` and must never be installed at all.

The second one-way door in this phase — the shape of migration 001 — is gated inside `01-02-PLAN.md`, immediately before the task that authors it, so the approval and the authoring share one context.

Purpose: an autonomous executor must not install a package nobody looked at, and must not reintroduce a deprecated one out of habit three plans later.
Output: `01-DECISIONS.md`, read by every later plan that installs anything.
</objective>
